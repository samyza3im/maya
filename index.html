01 — THE STUDIO

Designed
for living.

Every space has a character. Our role is to discover it, refine it and turn it into an environment that feels truly yours.

NOVA is an independent interior design studio focused on contemporary residential and commercial spaces.

We believe beautiful interiors are not created by following trends. They are created by understanding the people who live and work inside them.

From spatial planning and material selection to lighting, furniture and 3D visualization, every element is considered as part of one coherent story.
